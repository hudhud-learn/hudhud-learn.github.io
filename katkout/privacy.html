Privacy policy · Katkout

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short

Katkout is an app for children aged 4 to 9 to learn English or German. It works without an account, never shows ads, and has no analytics or tracking. Everything about your child stays on this device and is never sent anywhere. The one exception: if you subscribe to «Katkout Plus», the subscription service receives the store receipt with an anonymous ID, and nothing about the child.

Who is responsible?

Ahmad Yaghi. Contact: ahmadyaghi20151@gmail.com

What is stored, and where?

On this device only: stars, finished adventures and learned words for each language, and the settings you choose (the language your child learns, light or dark mode, daily timer). We run no servers that receive this data and cannot see it.

Microphone

Used only in «Say after me» when the child taps the microphone, so they can hear their own voice next to the word. The recording stays on the device, is deleted after playback, and is never sent or analysed. You can refuse the permission; the app keeps working.

What we never do

Never any ads, no advertising ID, no analytics, no tracking, no sign-in, no sharing or selling of any data, and no outside links a child can reach without the parents' gate.

Children (COPPA, GDPR-K and Google Play Families)

The app is made for children and is listed as a family app. We collect no personal information from children, so no consent for collection is needed. Settings, the subscription and the parents' report are behind the parents' gate (a sum for adults).

Katkout Plus (subscription)

Free: the first 3 worlds in full, 20 minutes a day (a new day starts at midnight by your device's clock) and book A01. «Katkout Plus»: every world, book and call with no daily limit, with a monthly or yearly subscription; the price in your currency is shown by the store before you buy. There is no lifetime option. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Buying, restoring and cancelling are behind the parents' gate only; payment and cancellation run through the App Store or Google Play, and we never see payment details. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Anything the child has finished stays open for review.

Subscription service (RevenueCat)

To verify the subscription we use RevenueCat. It receives the store receipt and an anonymous installation ID only; it receives nothing about the child or how the app is used, and is not used for ads or tracking. In a version without subscriptions nothing is sent.

Deleting data

Parents' area → «Delete all data», or delete the app. Everything is erased from the device at once.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date is written here and on the store page.
