Privacy policy · Katkout

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short

Katkout teaches English to children aged 4 to 9. It works without an account, without ads and without any analytics or tracking. Everything about your child stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.

Who is responsible?

Ahmad Yaghi. Contact: ahmadyaghi20151@gmail.com

What is stored, and where?

On this device only: stars, finished adventures, learned words, and the settings you choose (accent, light/dark mode, daily timer, children's nicknames if you type them). We run no servers that receive this data and cannot see it.

Microphone

Used only in «Say after me» when the child taps the microphone, so they can hear their own voice next to the word. The recording stays on the device, is deleted after playback, and is never sent or analysed. You can refuse the permission; the app keeps working.

What we never do

No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics, no sign-in, no sharing or selling of any data, and no outside links a child can reach without the parents' gate.

Children (COPPA and GDPR-K)

The app is made for children. We collect no personal information from children, so no consent for collection is needed. Settings, links and purchases are behind the parents' gate.

Deleting data

Parents' area → «Delete all data», or delete the app. Everything is erased from the device at once.

Subscription (later)

There are no in-app purchases yet. When «Katkout Plus» launches, payment and cancellation run only through the App Store or Google Play; we never see payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date is written here and on the store page.
