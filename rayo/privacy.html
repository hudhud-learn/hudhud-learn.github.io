Privacy in Rayo

In short: Rayo works without an account, collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere. Your progress stays on your phone.

What is stored, and where?

Only on your phone: your level, chosen situation, daily listening time, the sentences you heard, saved or marked as hard, your exercise and exam results, and your settings. We cannot see this data and it is never sent to a server.

Microphone

In shadowing you can record your voice to hear it next to the original sentence. The recording is kept only temporarily on your phone and is never uploaded. You can deny microphone access; everything else keeps working.

Internet and audio packs

The situations inside the app work offline. When you ask for the audio of a new situation or level, the app downloads audio packs from GitHub servers and sends no personal information. Like any website, GitHub may log your phone’s IP address during the download. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings → «Delete progress» erases all your data in the app. Uninstalling the app erases it too.

Children

Rayo is made for adults living in Germany and collects no data from any user, of any age.
