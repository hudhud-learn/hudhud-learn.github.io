Privacy in Zajel

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

In short

Your progress, notebook and writing are stored on your device only. We ask for no account and send no personal information. No ads, no tracking or analytics tools.

Who is responsible?

Ahmad Yaghi
Email: ahmadyaghi20151@gmail.com

What is stored, and where?

On your device only: the stories you read, vocabulary cards and review dates, what you write, and your settings. We run no servers that receive this data.

Internet and audio files

The core levels work offline. When you ask for the audio of an additional level, the app downloads audio files from GitHub servers and sends no personal information. Like any website, GitHub sees your device's IP address during the download.

Permissions

The app does not use the microphone, location or the advertising ID. The daily reminder is optional and is a local notification from your phone, without any server.

Backup of your progress

«Backup of my progress» writes a file on your phone and hands it to the system share sheet; you choose where it is saved. The file never reaches us.

External links

On the «Sources» screen you can tap an image-source link to open it in your browser. This is your own action, and the app sends no data.

Deleting data

Settings → «Delete my data», or delete the app. Everything is erased from the device.

Children

The app collects no data from any user, children included.
